His life

  1. 1896Born in Calcutta.
  2. 1922Meets his spiritual master, Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī.
  3. 1944Starts the magazine Back to Godhead.
  4. August 1965Sails to America on the Jaladuta.
  5. July 1966Incorporates the International Society for Krishna Consciousness in New York.
  6. January 1967Arrives in San Francisco.
