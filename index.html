Eight ways into the Vani Temple

Eight design directions for the new Vanipedia, for the meeting of 10 October 2026. Each has an entry page for the Vani Temple, leading to the four petals, and a Vanipedia main page in the same style.

  • Real photographsEach photograph is real, with its author and licence on the page. Nothing was generated.
  • No motion by itselfPages move only when you scroll. With reduced motion they are still.
  • Phone and computerNo rotation, no custom cursor, no overlap.
  • Search firstA real search form on every page. Small, fast pages.

About these prototypes

    
Content is real: the welcome text, titles and links come from vanipedia.org, vanisource.org, vaniquotes.org and vanimedia.org as they were on 8 October 2026. Links lead to the live pages where they exist; others are marked as prototype links. Visnu Murti Prabhu's wording for the title and the order of "Śrīla Prabhupāda in the Media" above "Disciples Remember Śrīla Prabhupāda" are used on every main page.

    
The pages were checked in Chromium at phone and computer widths, with and without reduced motion. They have not yet been checked on a real phone, in Safari or in Firefox. They carry noindex, so search engines leave them out.
