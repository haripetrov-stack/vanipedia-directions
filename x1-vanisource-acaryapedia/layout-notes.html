Acaryapedia page layouts: final draft

Notes for Vishnu Murti Prabhu, Kashya Prabhu and Mahendra Prabhu, with the pages shown side by side with Vanisource.

Design

A modern, easy-to-use design of its own: search first, the text in one clear main column, and the parts of every verse page always in the same order.

Śrīla Prabhupāda's photograph

On Vanisource his photograph stays on every page, as it has for years: in the right-hand column on a computer and in a strip under the title on a phone.

On Acaryapedia each page names the work and its author as text. A portrait appears only where one exists, on the author's own page.

Page types

Main page: search first, then where to begin and the list of authors.

Author page: who the author is, in a few lines, and the list of works.

Work page: the title, the author, one line about the work, the list of verses or chapters with their opening words, and the commentaries in this edition.

Verse page: the verse, word for word, translation, the commentaries (each one opens and closes), Śrīla Prabhupāda's translation and purport as a link to Vanisource, and the source at the very bottom.

Text

The original is shown in IAST (romanized Sanskrit or Bengali) with English. Devanāgarī and Bengali script are not shown on the page.

Where no published translation can be used, the English is a machine translation, labelled as such on the page and open for comments and corrections.

Sources are given once, at the bottom of the page. Checking states are kept for the editors and are not shown to readers.

What stays out

No dates of releases, plans or roadmaps, no scan pages and no page-flipping, no explanatory slogans. Vanisource and Vanipedia appear as links, not as a family of sites.

Speed and phones

Phone first. System fonts only, no web fonts and no video, so every page stays light and opens quickly on a slow connection.
