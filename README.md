# Vanipedia: design directions for the new site

Design prototypes for the new vanipedia.org, prepared for the meeting of 10 October 2026.

**Open the comparison page:** https://haripetrov-stack.github.io/vanipedia-directions/

Each direction has two pages: an entry page that leads to the four sites (Vanipedia, Vanisource, Vaniquotes, Vanimedia), and a Vanipedia main page in the same style. Open the pages, pick a first, second and third choice, add a note and send it to Pitambara (the page has a button that copies your choice).

There are three groups of directions, and one set of side-by-side mock-ups:

- **1 to 5, simple and fast:** easy to use and quick to load, with readable type, Śrīla Prabhupāda first and clear paths to his books, lectures, audio and video. Nothing moves.
- **6 to 13, with scroll effects:** each entry page is an experience that moves as you scroll.
- **14 to 16, bold experiments:** huge words as the menu that move as you scroll, one board of tiles, and a page that is only a search box.
- **17, Vanisource and Acaryapedia (20 October 2026):** mock-ups of Vanisource pages (main page, a book, a verse) with Śrīla Prabhupāda's photograph on every page, side by side with the same pages of Acaryapedia, and the notes on the Acaryapedia page layouts: https://haripetrov-stack.github.io/vanipedia-directions/x1-vanisource-acaryapedia/

About these prototypes:

- Every photograph is a real one with a free licence from Wikimedia Commons; its author and licence are shown on the page. Nothing was generated.
- Nothing moves by itself. In 6 to 14 pages move only when you scroll; with reduced motion they are still and complete.
- Text, titles and links come from the live Vanipedia sites as they were on 8 to 10 October 2026. Links without a known page are marked as prototype links.
- Checked in Chromium at phone and computer widths. Not yet checked on a real phone, in Safari or in Firefox.
- The pages carry `noindex`, so search engines leave them out.
