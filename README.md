# Vanipedia: eight directions for the new site

Design prototypes for the new vanipedia.org, prepared for the meeting of 10 October 2026.

**Open the comparison page:** https://haripetrov-stack.github.io/vanipedia-directions/

Each direction has two pages: an entry page for the Vani Temple, leading to the four petals (Vanipedia, Vanisource, Vaniquotes, Vanimedia), and a Vanipedia main page in the same style. Open the pages, pick a first, second and third choice, add a note and send it to Pitambara (the page has a button that copies your choice).

About these prototypes:

- Every photograph is a real one with a free licence from Wikimedia Commons; its author and licence are shown on the page. Nothing was generated.
- Pages move only when you scroll, never by themselves. With reduced motion they are still and complete.
- Text, titles and links come from the live Vanipedia sites as they were on 8 October 2026. Links without a known page are marked as prototype links.
- Checked in Chromium at phone and computer widths. Not yet checked on a real phone, in Safari or in Firefox.
- The pages carry `noindex`, so search engines leave them out.
